About us

Madada Events strategically position itself as an entity that rubberstamps the original roots and traditional culture to the young generation, a generation that has been largely grown in modernity leaving behind their heritage.

OUR THEME:

Celebrating Culture, Talent, and Community.

CORE OBJECTIVES:

  1. To celebrate and promote the rich Luhya culture.
  2. To create platforms for youth to showcase talent in music, art, and entrepreneurship.
  3. To stimulate local economic activity through business opportunities for vendors, artists and service providers.

After a highly successful 1st Edition of Vulai Festival at the KICC Nairobi on 2nd August 2025 which attracted 2,834 attendees and the 2nd Edition dubbed Ingoo Edition held on 27th Dec 2025 in Kakamega Showground that attracted 7,320 attendees we are now gearing up for the next editions; bigger, better and more impactful.

Safety and Security Measures

As a company, safety and security of our attendees; both clients, partners, guests and service providers, is at the core of our event organizing and execution processes.

Our ticketing processes are concrete to ensure that only those with valid ticket have a right to access the venues. Our marketing campaigns loudly sensitize our target customers on the safety measures taken and the unauthorized materials within and around the event venues.

We also partner with private security firms who strictly man the entry points and the fencing areas. We also do thorough screening for both individuals and vehicles to curb any entry of unauthorized substances and drinks and meals from outside.
